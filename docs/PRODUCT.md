# FanMesh product brief

## The problem

A follower count is not an owned audience. A creator can have a large visible following while only a small fraction receives a post, and each platform keeps a separate view of the relationship. Creators cannot reliably identify the people who care most, reach them with consent, or understand which actions caused a stream, sale, ticket purchase, or community contribution.

## Product promise

**Know your real fans. Own the connection. Activate genuine momentum.**

FanMesh becomes the identity and intelligence layer between a creator and the platforms they use. It does not replace those platforms; it helps the creator turn fragmented attention into a portable, permissioned relationship.

## Core product loop

1. **Connect** — ingest creator-authorized API data, webhooks, data exports, commerce events, and direct-channel consent.
2. **Resolve** — merge records only through strong identifiers or explicit fan confirmation; preserve provenance and confidence.
3. **Understand** — calculate transparent scores, cohorts, content affinity, recency, and lifecycle stage.
4. **Activate** — recommend the right segment, channel, timing, creative angle, and frequency cap.
5. **Learn** — attribute responses to a release or campaign and update recommendations without using sensitive traits.

## First production milestone

### Creator workspace

- Email or passkey authentication
- Creator/team organizations and roles
- Onboarding checklist and source health
- Audience dashboard with real data freshness

### Audience system

- PostgreSQL fan, identity, consent, event, campaign, and attribution records
- CSV/data-export importer with field mapping and duplicate preview
- Fan confirmation link for cross-platform identity linking
- Consent center, unsubscribe, export, and deletion workflows
- True Fan Score with model versioning and per-score explanations

### Activation

- Branded smart-link and early-access pages
- Email provider connection first; optional compliant SMS connection
- Segment builder with exclusion rules and frequency caps
- Release playbook with tracked links and conversion events
- Supported social publishing integrations where official APIs permit it

### Provider rollout

FanMesh completes one measurable provider loop before widening the surface area:

1. **Meta foundation** — Facebook Pages, Instagram professional insights, Meta ad accounts, Instant Forms, token lifecycle, authorized lead import, and attribution established the shared provider contract.
2. **Snapchat Business now** — corrected organization/ad-account discovery, visible activation diagnostics, Lead Generation Form inventory, creator-attested historical CSV normalization, HMAC-verified live submissions, replay protection, deduplication, and consent provenance; campaign reporting follows on the same adapter.
3. **Expansion lane** — TikTok for Business, Google/YouTube Ads, X Ads, and Threads reuse the same OAuth, sync-health, consent-provenance, and attribution contract.

The provider cards and OAuth foundation may appear before a provider is production-complete. FanMesh labels that state honestly instead of presenting a configured login button as a finished data integration.

## Later differentiators

- **Fan Passport:** fans voluntarily connect the places where they follow, listen, buy, and participate.
- **Momentum Window:** predicts when a specific fan cohort is most likely to respond, then stages a release sequence.
- **Affinity Graph:** maps themes, formats, products, and songs to observed first-party behavior.
- **Portable Community:** creator-defined fan circles that survive any single platform.
- **Collaborative Drops:** privacy-safe overlap insights for two creators without exposing either creator's raw fan list.
- **Music mode:** release cohorts, save/stream link attribution, superfans, city demand, merch/ticket signals, and catalog revival playbooks.

## Success metrics

- Percentage of followers converted into identified fans
- Percentage of identified fans with a direct opt-in
- Seven- and thirty-day reachable audience
- Campaign response and conversion by segment
- Identity merge precision and disputed-merge rate
- Unsubscribe, complaint, and frequency-cap violation rates
- Revenue or meaningful actions per 1,000 reachable fans

## Trust boundaries

FanMesh competes by making real fan relationships portable and actionable. Scraping, account intrusion, fake engagement, ranking manipulation, and unsolicited messaging are prohibited product directions.
