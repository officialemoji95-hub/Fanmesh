Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

FanMesh helps creators unify audience signals they are authorized to access. We use official platform APIs, creator-provided exports, and consented first-party data. We do not ask for social-media passwords, secretly scrape accounts, or sell personal information.

1. Scope

This policy describes how the FanMesh service processes information for creators who create an account, connect an authorized social or advertising account, import consented audience records, or use audience insights and activation planning.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, display name, workspace membership, and authentication records.
  • Authorized platform information: platform account identifiers, profile details, approved pages or business assets, aggregate audience and content metrics, and the permissions granted through an official OAuth consent screen.
  • Audience records: contact or campaign data a creator imports only when the creator confirms authorization and records the source, time, and type of consent.
  • Service activity: connection status, sync history, experiments, security events, and basic technical logs needed to operate and protect FanMesh.

3. How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, operate workspaces, connect approved accounts, synchronize requested data, deduplicate authorized audience records, produce explainable fan scores and recommendations, measure campaigns, prevent abuse, troubleshoot the service, and comply with law. FanMesh does not promise or manufacture platform reach, ranking, followers, views, or engagement.

4. Platform connections and tokens

Connections use official platform APIs and consent screens. FanMesh stores returned access and refresh tokens encrypted on the server and uses them only for the permissions the account owner approved. We never receive the user's platform password. A creator can disconnect a platform in FanMesh and can also revoke access from the platform's own settings.

5. Sharing and service providers

We disclose the minimum information needed to infrastructure and database providers that operate FanMesh, and to a social platform when an authorized user requests an API action for that platform. Current infrastructure may include Render and Supabase. We may also disclose information when legally required, to protect users and the service, or as part of a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards. We do not sell or rent audience data.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep information only as long as needed to provide the service, meet security and legal duties, and resolve disputes. Disconnecting a platform stops future synchronization and removes active credentials according to our retention process. Account owners may request deletion of their workspace and associated personal information through the support route below. Some limited records may be retained when required by law or for fraud and security prevention.

7. Security

FanMesh uses access controls, encrypted transport, encrypted OAuth credentials, short-lived authorization state, and workspace-level data separation. No service can guarantee absolute security, so account owners should protect their FanMesh and connected-platform accounts and report suspected misuse promptly.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on location, individuals may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or export personal information, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent. A creator must honor the rights of people in any audience data they upload. To make a request, use the FanMesh support route below and provide enough information for us to verify the relevant account or record.

9. International processing and children

Service providers may process data in countries other than the user's country, with protections appropriate to the transfer. FanMesh is intended for adult creators and business users and is not directed to children.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the product changes. We will revise the date above and provide additional notice when required. Privacy questions or deletion requests can be opened through the FanMesh project's public support channel.
