Official data download

Mesh followers without Meta business verification

Unzip the data download from Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube, then select the follower JSON or CSV files. FanMesh reads them locally, imports large audiences in safe batches, and keeps every identity platform-only.

  • Select follower, friend, or subscriber files—not messages, passwords, cookies, or a whole ZIP.
  • Usernames and profile links become identity signals, not email/SMS or automated-DM permission.
  • Repeat downloads update matching identities instead of creating copies.
